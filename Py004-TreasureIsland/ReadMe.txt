# 🏴‍☠️ Treasure Island
A beginner-friendly, text-based adventure game built with Python. Make choices, navigate dangerous situations, and try to discover the hidden pirate treasure!

## ✨ Features
- Interactive text-based gameplay.
- Multiple decision paths.
- Three stages of player choices.
- Different winning and losing outcomes.
- ASCII art introduction.

## 🎮 How to Play
Your adventure consists of three decisions:
1. **The Dock:** Choose whether to sneak past the guards or wait until nightfall.
2. **The Storm:** Decide whether to continue sailing or stop at a nearby island.
3. **The Doors:** Choose between the red, blue, and yellow doors to find the treasure.
Choose wisely! Not every decision leads to victory.

## 🛠️ Requirements
- Python 3.x
- No external libraries required.

## 🚀 How to Run
Navigate to the project directory and execute:
```bash
python main.py
```

## 💻 Example Gameplay
```text
Welcome to Treasure Island!

Your mission is to find the hidden pirate treasure.

You begin your journey at a dock guarded by the navy.
What do you do?
Wait until nightfall or sneak your way through?
(S - Sneak / W - Wait): w

Good choice! The guards got sleepy, and you passed through...

You set off on your journey, but the weather seems bad.
What do you do?
Continue sailing or stop at the nearby island?
(S - Stop at the island / C - Continue sailing): s

Smart choice! But now you must settle for the treasure
on this island, as you have no resources for a longer journey...

You stand before three doors...
A red door, a blue door, and a yellow door.
Only one leads to the treasure. Choose wisely!
(R - Red / B - Blue / Y - Yellow): y

Well, aren't you a lucky sailor! You found the treasure...
YOU WIN!
```
